The model

Prestige comes from the reference.

We optimize for one thing: that your work is permanently findable and citable, in literature reviews and in AI answers alike. The engine behind the reviews is honest incentive, out in the open.

01

Permanent DOIs

Every article is registered with Crossref and openly licensed under CC BY 4.0 — free to read, free to cite, indexed for search and AI answer engines.

02

Review that is itself citable

Opt-in open peer review: a reviewer's named critique is published alongside the article with its own citable DOI. A sharp public review builds the reviewer's own record.

03

A human always in the loop

Nothing publishes without human sign-off. Experts land the few high-level judgments only they can make; the mechanical work is handled for them.
