Author Guidelines

Scope & length

Articles are concise. Target 1,500–4,000 words. Longer only where the material demands it.

Structure

  • A structured abstract.
  • Clear sections appropriate to the article type.
  • Declarations: funding, conflicts of interest, AI-assistance disclosure, and data/code availability.

Reproducibility

Where an article makes an empirical or computational claim, the supporting data and code are deposited (e.g. Zenodo) with a DOI, and the article links to it.

Licensing

All articles are published under CC BY 4.0. By submitting, authors agree to this licence and retain copyright.

Submission

Submit via the journal's intake (form or email). Flow: acknowledgment → review → decision → publication. Founder-authored submissions follow the independent-handling rule in the editorial policy.

Novelty scoping (required)

Do a literature review and claim only what is genuinely new on your side. Cite the prior work, name what others have done, and scope your contribution to the part that is yours. It is fine to publish work whose building blocks exist elsewhere, as long as it moves the open domain forward (a measured, reproducible result is a contribution even when the primitives are known). Overclaiming is a reason for revision.

Implementation, contact & code (standard section)

Because we publish reusable building blocks, every article should tell readers how to use the work: a contact for implementation (or an offer to recommend collaborators), and — by default — a link to the finished code/repo so readers can actually get the thing. Omit the repo link only when publishing it would give away a real competitive advantage; that call is the author's, per article. Provide it in the article front matter:

implementation:
  contact: "how to reach the authors for implementation"
  repo: "https://github.com/...   # omit if competitively sensitive"
  note: "one line on what the repo contains"

Metadata

Authors provide full names, affiliations, and (recommended) ORCID iDs, so that DOI and indexing metadata are complete and correct.
