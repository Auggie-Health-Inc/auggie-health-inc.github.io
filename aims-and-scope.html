Aims & Scope

Augmented Intelligence publishes concise, rigorous work on human+AI collaboration — applied studies, methods, verdicts, and case reports where artificial intelligence augments, rather than replaces, professional judgment. We emphasize medicine, clinical operations, and research and professional workflows.

We favor short, bite-sized, reproducible contributions over long-form reviews. Every article discloses its AI-assisted methods transparently, and names the human who is accountable for the content.

Article types: Research · Methods · Verdict/Analysis · Case Report · Commentary.

How we work: we are openly AI-assisted with human final review. A human is always in the loop and nothing publishes without human sign-off; an expert reviewer's few high-level critiques are what shape the work, and AI handles the mechanical rest. We state this on every article.

Why review for us: the scarcest thing in research is sharp expert critique, so we make reviewing worth it. Open review is opt-in and credited — a reviewer's named critique can be published alongside the article with its own citable DOI, so their judgment becomes part of their own scholarly record. Good critique builds the reviewer's brand, not just the author's.

Open access: every article is published open access under a Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY 4.0) licence; authors retain copyright.
