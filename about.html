About

Augmented Intelligence is an open-access scholarly journal published by Auggie Health Inc. (Omaha, Nebraska). It publishes concise, citable work on human+AI collaboration, with every article openly licensed (CC BY 4.0), assigned a permanent Crossref DOI, and built to be found and cited.

Our premise is in the name: the strongest work now comes from humans and machines working together, with a human accountable for every claim and the AI assistance disclosed in the open. See our Aims & Scope, Editorial & Peer-Review Policy, and Author Guidelines.
